# TR sticker sheet ("Look what we hang on the wall")

George's badge poster, rebuilt with the real Discord role art (11 Nov 2026). Not served by the website; kept here as the source of record.

- `index.html`: the animated poster. Open it in a browser; fonts load from `fonts/`, so it works offline. `phone.html` frames it at 390 px.
- `img/`: badge art. `booster.svg` is George's pink candle, redrawn so it can move. `lp-anim.svg`, `citizen-anim.svg` and `guild-leader-anim.svg` carry the chart motion: an equaliser, and candles that rise green and fall red.
- `out/`: Codex renders.
  - one seamless 4.8 s GIF per badge (256 px), plus a 64 px Discord GIF and a WebP of each;
  - `poster-web` / `poster-phone` as MP4 and GIF;
  - `contact.png`;
  - `README.md` / `validation.json` with sizes and checks.
- `web3-logo/`: the WEB3 Masterclass logo round (A/B/C, size proof, Digital Walkers square badges). George picked **B (Node hex)**; it is live on the site as `public/logos/programs/web3-masterclass.svg`.
- `before-george-sent.png`: the sheet as George first sent it.

George's picks: Pathfinders gets the gold M/hawk; WEB3 gets its own Node hex badge; booster keeps the pink candle; prices dropped from Citizen; Wealth Builder named as the bundle; course cards name their Discord roles; booster rank line removed; new Community Leader wording; Eternals stays "Lifetime Citizen".
